## Intent

A TikTok ad for **The 90-Day Battle Plan** — a structured 90-day recovery plan for
Christian men quitting porn and compulsive masturbation. The ad is built around a
single emotional beat the buyer already knows: the 2 a.m. relapse loop, and the
shame that follows it.

Tone in the user's words: **raw, authentic, fast — not glossy.** The hook must land
in the first 1 second. Assume **sound-off viewing** — the burned-in captions carry
the entire message; the audio bed is atmosphere, not information.

The turn happens at 9s: the chains snap. Everything before it is tension and
self-recognition; everything after it is relief and a concrete offer.
