## Generated / built-in visuals

Every other visual in this video is authored in HTML/CSS — no photographic assets,
by hard constraint (no real or recognizable people, symbolic and atmospheric only).
The recurring visual system:

- **Phone-glow spill** — a cold off-white pool of light bleeding into near-black,
  standing in for the 2 a.m. screen. Never shows a face.
- **Chain links** — SVG-drawn iron links, low-key lit, used as the through-motif.
- **Ember burst** — radial white-hot core with orange/gold spark spray, the visual
  payoff at the 9s break. Matches the cover.
- **Dawn field** — a warm gold gradient wash replacing the cold blue, the relief beat.
